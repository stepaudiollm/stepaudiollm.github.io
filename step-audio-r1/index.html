Overview

StepAudio R1 builds on the architecture of StepAudio 2 and is designed specifically for audio-based reasoning. The model comprises an audio encoder, an audio adaptor, and an LLM decoder. It adopts the Qwen2 audio encoder, pretrained on diverse speech and audio understanding tasks, whose 25 Hz outputs are passed through a downsampling audio adaptor—identical to that of StepAudio 2—to produce 12.5 Hz latent features. These features are then consumed by a Qwen2.5 32B decoder, which generates reasoning chains followed by the final textual response. A central innovation is Modality-Grounded Reasoning Distillation (MGRD), which iteratively transforms initially semantic reasoning into "native audio think" by strengthening the model's grounding in acoustic cues, ensuring that reasoning is tied to the audio itself rather than its transcription alone. The model is pretrained using the same data and procedures as StepAudio 2, and subsequently refined through a dedicated Post-Training phase.

Abstract

Recent advances in reasoning models have demonstrated remarkable success in text and vision domains through extended chain-of-thought deliberation. However, a perplexing phenomenon persists in audio language models: they consistently perform better with minimal or no reasoning, raising a fundamental question—can audio intelligence truly benefit from deliberate thinking? We introduce StepAudio-R1, the first audio reasoning model that successfully unlocks reasoning capabilities in the audio domain. Through our proposed Modality-Grounded Reasoning Distillation (MGRD) framework, StepAudio-R1 learns to generate audio-relevant reasoning chains that genuinely ground themselves in acoustic features rather than hallucinating disconnected deliberations. Our model surpasses Qwen3 in textual reasoning while exceeding Gemini 2.5 Pro across comprehensive audio understanding and reasoning benchmarks spanning speech, environmental sounds, and music. These results demonstrate that reasoning is a transferable capability across modalities when appropriately anchored, transforming extended deliberation from a liability into a powerful asset for audio intelligence. By establishing the first successful audio reasoning model, StepAudio-R1 opens new pathways toward building truly multimodal reasoning systems that think deeply across all sensory modalities.
